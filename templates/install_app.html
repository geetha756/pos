📲 Install Sip & Snack

⬇️ Download the app
  1. Tap Download the app above.
  2. If a bar says "file may be harmful", tap Download anyway (it's your own app).
  3. Open the downloaded SipAndSnack.apk (pull down the notification bar if needed).
  4. Android asks to allow install from this source → allow → Install.
  5. Open Sip & Snack and sign in with your @sn15.ai account.
⚠️ Open this page in Chrome (or Samsung Internet) — not inside WhatsApp/Telegram or another app, or the download will get stuck.
Printer: pair the BT-58 in Android Settings → Bluetooth (PIN 0000 or 1234), then pick it in the app under Printer Settings.
